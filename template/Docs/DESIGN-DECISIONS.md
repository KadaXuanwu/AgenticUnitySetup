# Design decisions

Every game design and player-facing decision, by topic, as it stands now. One phrase per decision, its date, and under
it the problems it met and solved. An iterated choice keeps only its end state; what it replaced goes under it as a
problem. A reversal rewrites the entry in place and keeps the old choice as a problem line. Numbers are placeholders
unless the number is the decision. Tech and architecture choices: [AI/DECISIONS.md](../AI/DECISIONS.md).

Example of the form:

- **Players respawn at their own base** — 2026-01-01
  - a respawn at the point of death let a lost fight continue at once
